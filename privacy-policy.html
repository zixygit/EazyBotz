1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains what data the Gatekeeper bot (the "Bot") collects and how that data is used and protected. By using the Bot, you agree to the practices described in this Policy.

2. Data We Collect

The Bot may collect the following data:

  • Discord user IDs and usernames
  • Server (guild) IDs and server names
  • Channel IDs and channel names
  • Command usage data and message content required to execute commands
  • Logs for debugging, error tracking, and security

3. Why We Collect Data

We collect data to:

  • Provide and improve the Bot's features
  • Troubleshoot errors and maintain service reliability
  • Prevent abuse and enforce our Terms of Service

4. How Data Is Stored

Data is stored securely on the bot owner's personal machine. We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

5. Data Retention

Data is retained only as long as necessary to provide the service. Logs and command data are deleted after 30 days. You may request deletion of your data at any time.

6. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell your data. We do not share your data with any third parties, except:

  • Legal authorities if required by applicable law

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request a copy of data associated with your account
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt out of data collection by removing the Bot from your server

8. Children's Privacy

The Bot is not intended for users under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe we have collected such data, please contact us.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy at any time. Significant changes will be communicated through the Bot or Discord. Continued use of the Bot constitutes acceptance of the updated Policy.
