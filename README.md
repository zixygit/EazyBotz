**No coding. No complicated setup. No headaches.**

EazyBotz is a simple, user-friendly app that puts complete control of your Discord bot in your hands. Paste your bot token, and the entire interface unlocks — manage, monitor, and control your bot from one clean dashboard.

</div>

---

## 🚀 How It Works

```
1. Grab your bot token from the Discord Developer Portal
2. Paste it into EazyBotz
3. Done. The full control interface unlocks instantly
```

That's it. One input. Full power.

---

## 🔒 Security

**Your token is the key to your bot — guard it like one.**

- Never share your bot token publicly or commit it to any repository
- EazyBotz is designed to keep your token private and secure
- If your token is ever leaked, regenerate it immediately in the [Discord Developer Portal](https://discord.com/developers/applications)
